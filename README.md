\# IT Ticket Management Performance Analysis



\## Project Overview



This project analyzes IT ticket management performance from 2016 to 2020 using Microsoft Excel.



The analysis covers 97,498 IT support tickets handled by 50 IT agents. The project focuses on ticket volume, resolution time, satisfaction rate, request categories, issue types, severity, priority, and agent performance.



The objective is to identify operational bottlenecks and provide data-driven recommendations for improving IT support efficiency.



\---



\## Business Problem



The IT support team experienced significant growth in ticket volume while average resolution time remained relatively stable.



This project investigates:



\- Why ticket volume increased significantly

\- Which ticket categories create the largest resolution bottlenecks

\- How priority affects resolution time

\- Which agents may require additional training

\- Where automation can improve operational efficiency

\- What technology and process improvements should be prioritized

\## Key Findings



\### 1. Ticket Volume Growth



Ticket volume increased from 13,051 tickets in 2016 to 29,088 tickets in 2020, representing 123% growth.



\### 2. Ticket Routing Problem



29,410 tickets, representing approximately 30.2% of the total workload, were classified as unassigned.



This indicates a significant ticket prioritization and routing issue.



\### 3. Resolution Bottleneck



Hardware tickets had the highest average resolution time at 7.63 days, compared with 0.31 days for Login Access tickets.



\### 4. Agent Performance



12 agents were identified below the project satisfaction benchmark of 3.75, indicating potential opportunities for targeted training and coaching.



\### 5. Customer Satisfaction



Average satisfaction remained strong at 4.10/5 despite substantial growth in ticket volume.

\## Strategic Recommendations



1\. Implement automated ticket routing and priority assignment.

2\. Introduce targeted training for underperforming agents.

3\. Create a specialist fast-track process for Hardware tickets.

4\. Develop self-service capabilities for repetitive Login Access requests.

5\. Plan additional support capacity during predictable high-volume periods.

6\. Continuously monitor ticket volume, resolution time, and satisfaction.

\## Conclusion



The analysis shows that IT support demand increased substantially between 2016 and 2020 while resolution performance remained relatively stable.



The major opportunities identified are improving ticket routing, reducing Hardware resolution time, supporting underperforming agents through targeted training, and automating repetitive support requests.



This project demonstrates how Excel-based data analysis can be used to identify operational problems and translate quantitative findings into actionable business recommendations.
